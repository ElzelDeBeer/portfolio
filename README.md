##INTRODUCTION  
Project Name:  
#OOTD AI Chatbot (KERMS)

##Purpose and objective of the chatbot:  
The #OOTD AI Chatbot is an outfit of the day chatbot that helps a user choose outfits 
of the day on a daily basis or when they have an event and they do not know what to wear.
A user can add their clothing items - from shoes, clothes, jewelry/accessories and bags 
by inserting a direct link from the stores which the clothes were bought and/or by taking pictures
of what they currently have in their closet. The chatbot has a Pinterest Collab whereby it styles clothes from there.
As a user, you have the liberty to tell it the occasion and whether to consider certain outfits when it is giving out suggestions. It has a history to show the outfit combinations you have already worn according to the category, so if you search for occasion outfits twice, it will group it under the occasion folder. The occasion folder consists of categories such as corporate outfits, Parisian old money outfits, influencer events, day wear etc. will have their own group. On the top left side, there is a sidebar that consists of the following features: AI stylist, Historic Chats, Digital Wardrobe, Inspiration hub, Saved Outfits, Wear Logs, Profile, and Settings. I will outline what each feature/functionality does in the key functionalities section later in this document. 

##PROJECT OVERVIEW: 
Target audience:  
The #OOTD chatbot is catered for everyone, including any demographic and Living Standards Measure (LSM). 
This caters for new parents, influencers, corporate individuals, nano or macro fashion enthusiasts. 

##KEY FUNCTIONALITIES and FEATURES: 
The OOTD Chatbot includes several features designed to enhance the user's styling experience.
The AI Stylist provides personalized outfit recommendations, while the Digital Wardrobe allows users to manage their clothing inventory.
Users can access style ideas through the Inspiration Hub, save preferred looks using Saved Outfits, and monitor wardrobe usage through Wear Logs. 
The chatbot also maintains Historic Chats for easy reference and includes Profile and Settings sections for personalization and account management. 

##In-depth details of what each functionality does:  
The AI stylist feature is for user communication with the chatbot; here you can tell/ask the chatbot for any stylist suggestions.  
The Historic Chats feature is for viewing your previous suggestions or outfits you have worn.  
The Digital Wardrobe feature is where you can view Pinterest outfits; you can add a wardrobe item using a URL link directly from the store you purchased the outfit or from Pinterest.  
The Inspiration Hub feature is where you can discover curated fashion aesthetics and match them against your personal digital wardrobe.  
The Saved Outfits feature is where you get to save your favorite outfits either from the chatbot’s suggestions, or you can manually create and save a new board of outfits.  
The Wear Logs feature is where you can track when you wear outfits and inspect their original stylings chats. On the right side there is an option for you to either view the list in grid view or list view, and there is a log outfit worn. This is where a user can log their last worn outfit – consisting of the outfit title, date worn, occasion, vibe/aesthetic.  
The Profile feature is for user details. User's full name, email address, and personal style notes and color rules.  
The Settings feature is where a user can customize options for their preferred language, the chatbot’s appearance; 
either dark or light, the accent color of the chatbot depending on your mood. There is a plethora of options to choose from.

##SYSTEM ARCHITECHTURE: 
The OOTD Chatbot is a prompt-engineered AI solution that leverages a large language model to provide personalized outfit recommendations and styling assistance. 
The system consists of: 
• User Interface: Allows users to interact with the chatbot. 
• AI Model: Processes user prompts and generates outfit recommendations. 
• Prompt Engineering Layer: Defines the chatbot's personality, response style, and 		behavior. 
• User Data Inputs: Information such as style preferences, wardrobe items, 		occasions, and weather conditions that help personalize recommendations. 

##CHALLENGES and SOLUTIONS: 
Challenge:  
A challenge we encountered was not having enough credits for what we wanted the AI model to do,
so we had to go through a whole lot of different AI models until we got one that required a lot of credits to prompt our project.  
Solution: 
We tested a few models and finally went for Gemini; it successfully worked without any issues.  

Challenge: 
Maintain consistent response formatting. 
Solution: 
Implemented structured prompt guidance to ensure responses followed a predictable format.  

Challenge:  
Our chatbot had no chat input for the user. 
Solution:  
Used prompt engineering's best practice to refine and redesign the whole concept.
